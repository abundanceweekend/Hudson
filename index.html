The Gathering

Intention

We're gathering an intimate group of multifaceted individuals to come together in beautiful Hudson—to play, to learn, and to connect.

What is an interest of yours that you effortlessly continue to explore, and would like to share with others?

Each participant will share a passion or interest of theirs. Our current contributions include:

  • An architect sharing his process of creating and building his dream cottage right here on site.
  • A botanical watercoloring workshop as a way to mindfully observe and celebrate the garden.
  • A discussion centered on values-based investing—money, time, and energy.
  • An activity on the vitality of play-based movement (tailored to meet the needs of participants).
